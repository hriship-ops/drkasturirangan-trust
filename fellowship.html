FELLOWSHIP PROGRAMME

  • Nation-building through knowledge and education
  • Supporting research that transcends disciplinary boundaries
  • Financial support worth Rs 2 lakh for selected fellows
  • For anyone holding or pursuing a PhD
Applications open : October 25, 2026
Last date for submission : December 31, 2026
Objectives Benefits Expectations Eligibility Review process Timeline Application process

ABOUT THE FELLOWSHIP

Objectives

  • Supports emerging leaders committed to improving existing methods and systems to address major educational and societal challenges
  • Specifically targets individuals working on cross-disciplinary, interdisciplinary, or multidisciplinary topics that connect science, engineering, humanities, and social sciences

WHAT THE FELLOWSHIP PROVIDES

Fellowship benefits

Each Fellowship will receive a funding of ₹2 lakh for one year, with the possibility of renewal for an additional year.
Each fellow will be paired with a mentor, meeting regularly throughout the year to exchange ideas, discuss progress, and receive guidance.
There will be two workshops where fellows present their work for an informal review session with invited experts and peers.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Expectations from Fellows

Attend and actively participate in workshops and mentoring events over the period of fellowship

Present vision and updates on ongoing work during designated times

Uphold and demonstrate the values of Dr Kasturirangan in the professional sphere

Stay engaged with the Trust and support future cohorts of fellows

CURRENT CALL

Eligibility

Must have been awarded a PhD in the past X years or be in the final stages of a PhD from a recognised state institution.
All ages and all disciplines are welcome to apply
Must have demonstrated research ability through peer-reviewed publications or conference presentations.

HOW FELLOWS ARE CHOSEN

Review process

All applications will be reviewed to ensure they are complete and meet the eligibility criteria before being forwarded to the jury.
→
A distinguished jury of experts will evaluate the applications and prepare a shortlist based on merit and alignment with the trust’s values and objectives.
→
Shortlisted candidates will be notified, and interviews will be scheduled with a panel.
→
Selected fellows will be announced on April 25, 2027

KEY DATES

Timeline

Oct 25, 2026 Applications open
Dec 31, 2026 Last date of submission
March 2027 Interviews of shortlisted candidates
April 25, 2027 Fellows announced

HOW TO APPLY

Application process

Submit your details on the application portal by December 31, 2026.

Please keep these supporting documents handy at the time of submission:

Curriculum Vitae (maximum 2 pages)

Proof of PhD completion or current enrolment

Cultivating Excellence. Futures Thinking. Advancing Humanity.
