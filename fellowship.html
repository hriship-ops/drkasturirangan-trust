FELLOWSHIP PROGRAMME

  • Honouring Kasturirangan's legacy of nation-building through knowledge and education
  • Supporting research that transcends disciplinary boundaries
  • Financial support worth Rs 4 lakh for selected fellows

Please note that 2026 fellowship application is by nomination only. An invited panel will nominate candidates for consideration.

Nominations accepted from:
October 25, 2026

Last date for submission:
December 31, 2026

ABOUT THE FELLOWSHIP

Objectives

  • Supports emerging leaders committed to improving existing methods and systems to address major educational and societal challenges
  • Specifically targets individuals working on cross-disciplinary, interdisciplinary, or multidisciplinary topics that connect science, engineering, humanities, and social sciences

WHAT THE FELLOWSHIP PROVIDES

Fellowship benefits

Each fellowship will provide funding of ₹4 lakh for one year, with the possibility of renewal for an additional year.

Fellows will have opportunities to talk to/interact with eminent individuals across various fields, throughout the year to exchange ideas, discuss progress, and receive guidance.

Two online/physical workshops where fellows present their work and participate in an informal review session with invited experts and peers.

RESPONSIBILITIES

Expectations from Fellows

Attend and actively participate in workshops and mentoring events over the period of fellowship

Present vision and updates on ongoing work during designated times

Uphold and demonstrate the values of
Dr Kasturirangan in the professional sphere

Fellows from prior cohorts are encouraged to continue participating in the Trust's events to build and sustain a lasting community

CURRENT CALL

Eligibility

No self-nominations are allowed. Candidate must be nominated by someone from the invited pool of experts.
Nominated candidate must have submitted a PhD thesis between January 01, 2025 to December 31, 2026.
Nominated candidate must have demonstrated research ability through peer-reviewed publications or conference presentations.

HOW FELLOWS ARE CHOSEN

Review process

Invited individuals who knew Dr. Kasturirangan well and from selected central universities will nominate candidates.

→

A distinguished jury of experts will evaluate the applications and prepare a shortlist based on merit and alignment with the trust’s values and objectives.

→

Shortlisted candidates will be notified, and interviews will be scheduled with a panel.

→

Selected fellows will be announced on April 25, 2027

KEY DATES

Timeline

Oct 25, 2026 Nomination window opens
Dec 31, 2026 Last date of submission
March 2027 Shortlisted candidates invited for an interview
April 25, 2027 Fellows announced

HOW TO APPLY

Application process

Please remember that this is a closed call and candidates have to be nominated by an invited panel.

These are the documents that need to be submitted as part of the nomination:

Nomination letter on official letterhead and bearing original or authenticated signatures.

Tip for the nominator: The letter must demonstrate the nature of your relationship with the nominee and their familiarity with their work.

Candidate’s Curriculum Vitae (maximum 2 pages)

Proof of candidate’s PhD thesis submission

Candidate’s statement of purpose (maximum 1000 words) clearly outlining how their research aligns with the Trust’s objectives, their motivations, and how the funds can help them.

Tip for candidate: Try to demonstrate the interdisciplinarity of your work, its anticipated impact, and its relevance to academic, corporate, or public policy

Budget proposal outlining how the funds will be used by the candidate to support their proposed research and professional development.
