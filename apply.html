Please note that formal applications are only by nomination

The nomination application must include the following documents and must be sent to info@drkasturirangantrust.org by December 31, 2026.

1

Curriculum Vitae
(maximum 2 pages)

2

Proof of PhD completion or thesis submission between January 01, 2025 and October 23, 2026

3

Statement of purpose (maximum 1000 words) clearly outlining how your proposed research aligns with the Trust’s objectives; your motivations, and how the Fellowship funds would help you.

Tip: Try to demonstrate the interdisciplinarity of your work, its anticipated impact, and its relevance to academic, corporate, or public policy

4

A nomination letter from NOMINATING GROUP (TBD)

Tip for the nominator: The letter must demonstrate the nature of your relationship with the nominee and their familiarity with their work.

5

Two letters of endorsement/support by Institution Head/Professor/Senior collaborators who are familiar with you and your project.

Note: All letters must be on official letterhead and bear original or authenticated signatures.

6

Budget proposal outlining how the funds will be used to support your proposed research and professional development.
