Please remember that this is a closed call and candidates have to be nominated by an invited panel.

Please submit the following documents to
info@drkasturirangantrust.org

1

Nomination letter on official letterhead and bearing original or authenticated signatures.

Tip for the nominator: The letter must demonstrate the nature of your relationship with the nominee and their familiarity with their work.

2

Candidate’s Curriculum Vitae (maximum 2 pages)

3

Proof of candidate’s PhD thesis submission

4

Candidate’s statement of purpose (maximum 1000 words) clearly outlining how their research aligns with the Trust’s objectives, their motivations, and how the funds can help them.

Tip for candidate: Try to demonstrate the interdisciplinarity of your work, its anticipated impact, and its relevance to academic, corporate, or public policy

5

Budget proposal outlining how the funds will be used by the candidate to support their proposed research and professional development.

Applications with incomplete documentation will not be eligible for selection
